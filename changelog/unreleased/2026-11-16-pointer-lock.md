- `El::pointer_lock` holds the pointer on a press: the cursor hides and stays put, and the mouse's raw motion goes to the element as `[dx, dy]` points, past the window's and the screen's edges, until Escape, focus loss or the element going away. `El::pointer_lock_while_held` also lets go when the button comes up, for knobs and scrubbed numbers. `Cx::pointer_locked` and `Runtime::pointer_locked` say whether it's held; `Runtime::pointer_motion` and the script event `{"type": "pointer-motion", "dx", "dy"}` feed motion headless. Desktop windows only (#494).
